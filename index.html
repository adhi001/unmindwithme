A note from Pavithra

Let’s make room for your story.

Hello, I’m Pavithra Jayanthi, a Consultant Psychologist. I believe that understanding yourself can be a meaningful first step towards change — and that you deserve to approach that process with patience and compassion.

Unmind with Me is a welcoming space to explore your thoughts, emotional patterns, self-worth, and boundaries without pressure to have every answer straight away.

There is no need to tell your story perfectly. We can begin with what feels important to you, at a pace that feels manageable.
